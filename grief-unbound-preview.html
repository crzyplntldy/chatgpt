You Are Not Alone

Grief can feel like a weight no one else can see—and the world expects you to "move on."

Maybe it's the loss of someone you loved deeply. A relationship, a role, a version of yourself. Whatever brought you here—the sleepless nights, the hollow mornings, the sense that something essential has been torn away—your grief is real, and it deserves real support.

Short-term therapy or a single support group can help. But some grief needs more. It lives in the body, in the nervous system, in the spaces between words. It asks for something deeper.

That's exactly why Grief Unbound exists.

"Grief is the price we pay for love—and healing is the gift we give ourselves."

The Program

What Is Grief Unbound?

More than therapy. More than a support group. Grief Unbound is a holistic healing journey designed for those who are ready to transform their relationship with loss.

🌊

Body-Centered Healing

Grief doesn't just live in the mind—it's held in the body. We incorporate somatic awareness, gentle movement, and breathwork to help release what words alone cannot reach.

🧘

Mindfulness & Meditation

Learn evidence-based mindfulness practices that build resilience, regulate your nervous system, and help you be present with—rather than overwhelmed by—your grief.

🤝

Compassionate Community

Heal alongside others who truly understand. Our small, intimate groups create a container of safety, trust, and profound human connection.

✨

Integrated Wellness Modalities

Drawing from yoga, Reiki, expressive arts, and evidence-based grief therapy, Grief Unbound addresses healing on every level—mind, body, and spirit.

🏡

A Sacred Space

Held in our beautiful heritage house in Saddle River, NJ—a warm, peaceful sanctuary away from the noise of everyday life, purpose-built for deep healing work.

🎯

Expert Guidance

Facilitated by licensed mental health practitioners with specialized training in grief, trauma, and integrative healing modalities.

The Journey

What You'll Experience

Grief Unbound is a multi-dimensional program that meets you where you are and guides you toward wholeness.

01

Free Discovery Call

Begin with a complimentary conversation. We'll listen to your story, answer your questions, and help determine if Grief Unbound is the right fit for your healing journey.

02

Somatic Grief Exploration

Learn to listen to your body's wisdom. Through guided movement, breathwork, and body-awareness practices, you'll begin to release grief that's been held in the nervous system.

03

Mindfulness & Meditation Practices

Develop a sustainable mindfulness practice that supports your nervous system regulation, cultivates self-compassion, and builds the emotional resilience to face grief with presence.

04

Therapeutic Group Process

Share and be witnessed in a small, carefully held group. Experience the profound healing that comes from being truly seen—and from seeing yourself in others.

05

Integrative Healing Modalities

Experience the healing power of yoga, Reiki, and expressive arts—modalities that access healing pathways beyond cognitive processing alone.

06

Finding Meaning & Moving Forward

Guided by research from grief experts like David Kessler and Elisabeth Kübler-Ross, we'll explore how to carry your loved one's memory forward—with more love than pain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Grief Unbound is a holistic, immersive grief healing program at the Center for Mind Body Balance in Saddle River, NJ. It goes beyond traditional talk therapy to incorporate somatic (body-based) healing, mindfulness, yoga, Reiki, expressive arts, and compassionate group support. It's designed for those navigating significant loss who are ready for deep, lasting transformation—not just short-term coping.

Grief Unbound is for anyone experiencing significant grief—whether from the death of a loved one, the end of a relationship, a major life transition, loss of identity, or any other profound loss. It's especially suited for those who feel that traditional therapy alone isn't enough, and who are ready to engage in deeper, body-centered healing work.
